# Render and verify

**Durable jobs.** CLI renders, transcription and matte passes run through a runner that outlives the agent session. On macOS: `open -a Terminal job.command` (an adapted `templates/render.command` / `talking-head/render.command` with its scoped watchdog). Confirm it started; monitor its log and exit status. In zsh templates, `setopt null_glob` before wildcard cleanup. Cap ffmpeg at `-threads 4`. A historical 14-minute edit used about 20 GB of frame cache, so measure free space first.

Detail for SKILL.md phases 6–7. App export path: [hyperframes-app.md](hyperframes-app.md) §6.

## 8. Check → render → verify

- **REFERENCES.md is complete** (§5c) before any render.
- **Deliberate bumper timing passes §3a.** Before a full render, the cue record and encoded transition preview must show a complete phrase, a measured immediate handoff, and the intended next CTA. After rendering, verify that same boundary in the actual final MP4; retain before/after evidence for a correction.
- `npx hyperframes check` must pass (lint, layout, motion, WCAG contrast). Mark intentional layering with `data-layout-allow-overlap`, `data-layout-allow-occlusion` or `data-layout-ignore`.
- Snapshot contact sheets across the whole timeline and look at them (`npx hyperframes snapshot`, `--zoom` for close crops). When the app is open, MCP `preview.frame` gives single frames without a render.
- **Editorial pacing and speech-context QA are required in addition to codec QA.** Audit the entire final timeline, including the hook, every edit boundary, long unillustrated passage, references, CTA and ending. Check retained pauses against the actual audio and sentence meaning, recover ASR omissions using short source windows, and review the speech before and after every proposed trim. Preserve quiet word onsets, complete names, final consonants, natural breaths and coherent sentence/context transitions. Log removed pauses/restarters and justify deliberately retained longer gaps. Use evidence from the actual installed cut tools and their execution before changing detection logic; thresholds alone cannot establish that a gap is expendable. Re-transcribe the complete edited voice and investigate differences from the intended script; hear/watch when the tool surface permits and report limits honestly. Playback repetitions reported during live review may be viewer rewinds; verify them in the saved file before editing.
- **Ending timing passes its cue gate.** Check the complete signoff and immediately following fade in a bounded preview, then in the actual final MP4. All speech must finish before the fade begins; the picture/audio tail must end deliberately, without an idle hold or abrupt musical cutoff. Retain timing measurements and frame/audio evidence.
- **Render path.** Default: the app's Export after the creator's go ([hyperframes-app.md](hyperframes-app.md) §6), then copy the MP4 next to the source media. CLI path (long/matte-heavy mains, shorts batches, or when the creator asks): render `reference/templates/render.command` through the chosen durable runner:
  - **CLI version policy: test, then move the pin.** Record `hyperframes --version` in `BRIEF.md` for every build. Use the app-bundled CLI (0.8.137 at writing: `~/Library/Application Support/HyperFrames/agent-cli/bin/hyperframes`) or `npx hyperframes@latest` for `check`, `snapshot`, preview and app exports; a 10:42 main exported from the app on 0.8.137 in 21 min without a crash. Keep **0.8.81** (the last version proven on long renders in the reference setup) for CLI renders of long or matte-heavy mains until one such main passes on the newer version; then update this line. Verify flags with `render --help` before use. The general template uses `--workers 2`; the talking-head runner defaults to `VIDEO_RENDER_WORKERS=1` and `VIDEO_BROWSER_TIMEOUT=240`. Newer CLIs add `--frames-cache-dir` (point it at the external cache volume) and `--video-frame-format png` (sharper screen-demo text).
  - `HF_SEGMENTED_CAPTURE=true HF_SEGMENT_FRAMES=150 HF_SEGMENT_BROWSER_RECYCLE=1 HF_FORCE_DRAWELEMENT=1`
  - `--experimental-fast-capture=true --gpu --video-bitrate 20M --resume --keep-segments`
  - all caches external
  - **Resource watchdog:** `reference/talking-head/render.command` monitors boot disk and free RAM (historical defaults: 700 MB and 20%; configure for current capacity). It stops only its own render process group, records failure and never marks a stopped run done. Resume only after capacity recovers.
  - **Capacity and recovery:** a historical 14-minute edit used about 20 GB of frame-extract cache, so measure free space on the chosen cache volume before capture. If final segment joining fails, preserve kept segments; `reference/talking-head/manual_mux.py` illustrates joining them and rebuilding audio from the composition cues. Adapt frame rate, paths and supported ffmpeg flags, confirm segment completeness, and verify recovered picture/audio; cache removal requires authorization for the exact disposable directory.
  - **Encoded loudness:** `reference/talking-head/finalize.py` illustrates an audio-only correction with picture stream-copy and a QA report. AAC can overshoot the target; a lower input true-peak target (historically −2.0 dBTP) may be needed, followed by remeasurement of the encoded file against the final −1.5 dBTP ceiling.
  - main edit first, then the shorts
  - historical benchmarks (not a promise of present performance): 9 min of edit plus 4 shorts took 18 min; 14 min plus 4 shorts took 12 min. Memory stayed 39–62% free and the fan stayed quiet.
- **Verify every file:**
  - ffprobe duration and frame count match the plan
  - actual final video packet cadence passes `reference/scripts/media_clock.py` against the explicit rational FPS and EDL frame count; partial splices require common segment time bases, exact durations and full unaffected-frame/audio identity checks described in `reference/editorial-method.md`
  - full decode with 0 errors
  - EBU R128 loudness
  - sample frames from the real MP4
  - **QA gate passes (exit 0):** `python3 <skill-root>/tools/render-qa-gate/qa_gate.py <render.mp4> --script <intended words>` with `--expect 1920x1080` (main) or `--short` (shorts). It measures format, loudness floor, dead air, black/frozen video and whether every intended word is still audible, and writes `<render> QA/qa-report.md` beside the file. A fail goes back for a fix and through the gate again. It does not replace the checks above or the review of its `review-frames/`; captions and framing are not measured. Mark intended silences with `--allow START-END` and record them in the report-in.
- **Deliver `TRANSCRIPT.md`** with the renders: corrected names and final-video timestamps including the inserted bumper.
- Hand the creator the path to the renders folder. Verify the current delivery surface supports the files; always supply the actual saved paths.
