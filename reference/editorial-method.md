# Talking-head editing, independent of presentation style

This is the editing method the skill follows. Historical assertions supplied with the method are lessons, not proof that every earlier edit performed them.

1. **CUT → STRUCTURE → SKIN.** Decide what stays and where seams land from footage, words and meaning. Decide what the viewer sees moment by moment next. Apply the chosen colors, fonts and movement afterward. The skin must preserve editorial quality, pacing and explanatory coverage.
2. **Measure this recording.** Measure actual scene noise floor, loud speech, quiet consonants, pauses and loudness. Record the signal, units, window size, calibration spans and selected parameters. A threshold from another recording is a historical example. The prior copied −45 dB gate clipped quiet speech; the corrected same-footage −64 dB gate is comparison evidence, not a universal preset. Transcript chooses WHAT to cut; source waveform and actual quiet seams determine WHERE. Protect soft onsets and complete tails. Tighten confirmed silence with natural lead/tail air and justified rhetorical pauses. Review non-transcribed sound islands before merging across them; their origin may be unknown.
3. **Cut by meaning.** Remove unwanted fillers, false starts, retakes, tangents and editor instructions. Preserve jokes, intentional emphasis/repetition, rhythm and corrected meaning. Flag substantive uncorrected claims; do not invent replacement speech. Log content cuts in CUT-NOTES.md for veto. Verify every ASR gap over 1.5 seconds against actual audio and re-transcribe non-silent or uncertain gaps. Inspect unusually long ASR words and punctuation-only timing tails. Re-transcribe ALL finished edited voice, diff against intended retained words, investigate each missing/mangled/leftover phrase, and perform a second filler/restart pass. Source and timeline frame counts must agree.
4. **Diagnose sound before processing.** Use the minimum processing justified by the actual recording. Heavy denoising can pump or muffle; preserve natural consonants and breaths. Measure matched-loudness A/B windows, speech and pauses. State when subjective listening is unavailable. Keep the final soundtrack separate from the picture so audio fixes can use video stream-copy. Independently measure encoded main and EACH affected Short at −16 LUFS and ≤−1.5 dBTP; a normalized master does not establish excerpt loudness.
5. **SHOW and SAY.** Read the edited transcript sentence by sentence. SHOW numbers, names, comparisons, processes and visual references; SAY personal opinion, story and emotion on the presenter. When the source screen already explains the point clearly, preserve it and add helpful guidance instead of duplicating it. Give each graphic one memorable headline. Values and claims come from actual source screens/verified data; label concepts and qualifications explicitly.
6. **Motion follows information.** Match delivery energy. Give material readable dwell (roughly 1.6 seconds minimum for a simple graphic, longer for more text). Choose full camera, side graphics, presenter cards and source stages by content; avoid mechanical preset rotation. Protect face, head, hands and gestures over the entire movement window. Keep voice full level and effects low. Use designed transitions at hard layout seams. Slow passages need useful explanatory structure, not arbitrary decoration. Suggest a stronger hook when warranted; obtain user approval before changing the house format. Keep requested bumper and signoff handoffs precise.
7. **Assets and presenter fidelity.** Every spoken visual reference or diagram request must be shown, or its unavailability explained. Use real licensed/credited assets and correct names from actual frames. Graphics must be big, bold and readable on a phone. Never generate the presenter's likeness. Masking is selective, never dominant: original footage, full camera and rounded cards carry most of the edit; cut-outs are reserved for big emphasis beats, and a rounded original-footage card replaces any matte that looks chunky.
8. **Editorial plus technical QA.** Check the tools, frame-exact EDL, every actual encoded card/layout/transition, full decoding, encoded loudness, whole edited-audio transcript diff and leftover fillers. Recalculate chapters and captions from final audio after edits. Run heavy work serially with durable jobs and an own-process watchdog. Diagnose root causes and run regression checks across affected shared code/assets, including each affected Short. Unchanged Shorts may reuse their previous complete QA only after byte hashes and shared dependencies are verified unchanged. Report exactly what passed, failed or was not tested.
9. **Truthful packaging and limits.** Use a question title when there is no verdict. Keep unproven claims and prices out of titles/thumbnails; preserve qualifications. Do not invent an outro recommendation title. Tone, rhythm and dynamic taste inferred without listening are limitations; verify one-frame numbers/names at full resolution and report remaining uncertainty.

## Exercise the supporting checks

`reference/scripts/editorial_checks.py` is a standard-library helper; no package installation is needed. It validates evidence and reports differences; it does not choose substantive edits or claim subjective sound/retention quality.

- `edl --before OLD.json --after NEW.json --pause-window 102.5:105.03:benchmark-pause --report OUT.json`: validates frame/source order, expected inserted bumper frames, and the actual retained duration of a reviewed quiet span before/after. Use source spans measured for the current recording; the numbers here are the current project's regression example.
- `words --expected EXPECTED.json --actual ACTUAL.json --aliases ALIASES.json --report OUT.json`: diffs full word lists, ignoring punctuation tokens without extending word tails. Every mismatch requires source/final-window review. Re-run with `--reviews REVIEWS.json`; reviews must match current input hashes and include existing local evidence. Unreviewed differences fail.
- `cues --fps 24000/1001 --cue-end SECONDS --bumper-frame FRAME --signoff-end SECONDS --fade-frame FRAME --fade-end-frame FRAME --report OUT.json`: checks measured completion-to-splash gap and next-frame signoff fade; it does not measure acoustic endpoints for the editor.
- `shorts --baseline PREVIOUS_QA.json --count 4 --report OUT.json`: verifies unchanged vertical deliverable hashes and previous complete machine QA. Any changed file fails this reuse gate and needs fresh affected-Short QA.
- `visuals --plan VISUAL-BEATS.json --report OUT.json`: checks narration/purpose metadata, times, readable dwell warnings, encoded-frame evidence and coverage. Counts are observations, not a prescribed number of cards. View the frames and evaluate semantic usefulness/presenter protection; the script cannot establish those qualities.

A visual plan uses `{duration, evidence_video, beats:[{id,start,end,kind,phrase,purpose,headline,encoded_frames:[paths]}]}` with kind SHOW, SAY or BRAND. SHOW/BRAND beats require encoded frame evidence. SAY beats retain the presenter and state their narrative purpose. Evidence paths resolve relative to the plan. A word-review file uses `{expected_sha256, actual_sha256, reviews:{group_id:{status:"verified_asr_variation",notes,evidence:[paths]}}}`. Fix actual editing errors instead of approving them as ASR variation. Archive helper outputs, cue records, calibration, cut notes, full word diffs and before/after examples with the renders.

## Calibrated quiet-pass execution

Use `reference/scripts/calibrated_pause_pass.py CONFIG.json` with an existing local Python environment that already has NumPy; do not install packages implicitly. The script proposes source removals and writes its configured JSON report, without modifying media. Configuration supplies `rms_file` (a one-dimensional NumPy array of measured dB values), `rms_step_seconds`, rational-FPS numeric value `fps`, measured `gate_db`, `minimum_run_seconds`, positive `tail_handle` and `lead_handle`, retained source `ranges`, `exceptions`, `context_reviews`, and `output`. No threshold or handle is a universal preset. Paths in the configuration should be absolute. Record calibration and the RMS generation command separately.

Each `context_reviews` key is the measured candidate start formatted to three decimals; its value supplies actual review `notes` and existing local `evidence` paths. An otherwise eligible pause without this review remains `pending contextual review` and is not removed. `exceptions` entries keyed by candidate `start` can retain a rhetorical pause or select measured handles. Review uncertain sounds and speech context separately; this quiet pass never bridges a louder island. Apply approved frame-exact removals to the EDL, verify speech preservation, and run `editorial_checks.py edl` against the real before/after timeline. Keep unreviewed candidates out of the final cut, but don't leave them unreviewed: the default taste is tight, so review every candidate before the first preview (see the gap sweep in [cut-and-audio.md](cut-and-audio.md)).

## Short presenter intervals

Use `reference/scripts/face_interval.py` to select face-track samples inside each actual source interval. If a subclip is shorter than the tracker cadence and has no contained sample, use the source-time sample nearest the interval midpoint. Never fall back to the first recording frame. Empty tracking data requires source review, not a guessed crop. The helper takes JSON `{samples:[[time,cx,cy,w,h],...],start,end}` and returns selected samples; its importable `select_interval` has the same behavior. Verify the actual encoded seam frames, full heads and meaningful gestures after applying any crop. Preserve the old render when a reviewer is watching it and version the corrected result.

## Long visual holds need a real editorial review

A real review exposed a 38.8-second unillustrated section (00:56.6–01:35.4) that a generic SAY label and total card count had hidden. The visuals evidence check now reports the complement of useful SHOW/BRAND coverage across the entire timeline. Include all useful momentary explanatory overlays in the plan; a persistent channel header or captions alone do not count as an explanation.

`visuals --review-hold-seconds 15` uses 15 seconds as a configurable review trigger, not a maximum shot length or a required graphic frequency. Each longer graphic-free interval requires a specific `hold_reviews` entry with `start`, `end`, `reason`, and existing local `evidence` paths. Actual emotions, jokes, uninterrupted stories and original pointing may justify long full-camera holds. Read the narration and watch encoded frames before writing that reason; generic “opinion/emotion/transitions” boilerplate is insufficient. Add explanatory beats only where the content warrants them. Re-run the helper after new overlays and view their motion/readable dwell in the encoded result. In fast-cut mode the trigger is 6 s and `pacing_check.py` also counts punch-ins and layout changes (fast-cut-mode.md).

## Partial renders and final assembly

One repair exposed a real failure: original HyperFrames segments used a 1/90000 video time base, while finished replacement MP4s used 1/24000. Direct concat compressed replacement timestamps despite matching metadata frame counts. Preserve a failed candidate as a clearly labeled draft and correct the assembly without rerendering picture. Stream-copy remux replacement video-only segments to the same time base, supply each exact frame-count/FPS duration to concat, and mux original audio independently.

Run `reference/scripts/media_clock.py FINAL.mp4 --frames EDL_FRAME_COUNT --fps RATIONAL_FPS --report QA.json` after muxing. It checks every actual presentation timestamp and packet duration against the rational clock with only half a container tick allowed for rounding. Metadata duration/frame counts alone do not prove playback cadence. Full-decode both versions and compare every unaffected decoded frame hash; actual rewritten windows need visual/motion/seam review. Verify final AAC and decoded PCM identity before reusing previous complete edited-audio ASR/diff for a strictly picture-only revision. Any audio or EDL change invalidates that reuse. Preserve the file the reviewer is playing and clearly version the corrected output.


## Narration-driven illustrations and full-screen passages

Apply [bilateral-visual-storytelling.md](bilateral-visual-storytelling.md). SHOW and SAY may coexist: a symbolic process or narrated action can inhabit the negative space of a personal story while the face remains dominant. Settle illustrations during vulnerable delivery. Selected original illustrative takeovers may temporarily remove the presenter while their unchanged voice continues, then return for reaction or evidence. Plan object → spoken action → consequence; audit both side spaces and the whole video. Precisely align word-driven state changes and measured effect energy to visible motion. Execute `scripts/bilateral_checks.py` on the project evidence contract at authoring and final stages. Source PCM integrity requires independent retained-source mapping; same-WAV correlation is invalid evidence.
